# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Primary users are citizens of Slovenia and employees of organizations in Slovenia. Citizens use the app to securely receive official documents through smart lockers. Employee users use the app to manage document insertion and pickup through government-connected smart lockers. Both groups need a clear, trustworthy mobile experience that works under real-world conditions and does not require interpretation.

## Product Purpose
ePrevzem is a production mobile app for secure document pickup and operator locker workflows. It exists to make official document handoff feel dependable, traceable, and easy to complete on a phone. Success means users can understand their current state immediately, complete the next required action without confusion, and trust that the system is handling sensitive government processes correctly.

## Brand Personality
Government, clean, trust.

The product should feel formal, calm, and production-ready. It should read as a real Slovenian government-connected service, not a startup product, consumer fintech app, or generic SaaS template.

## Anti-references
- Startup gradients and attention-seeking visual effects
- Card-heavy dashboard layouts that feel like generic business SaaS
- Playful fintech styling, including overly friendly tone or flashy accents
- Glossy marketing UI that prioritizes presentation over clarity and trust

## Design Principles
- Trust through restraint: interfaces should feel stable, formal, and credible without becoming cold or bureaucratically dense.
- Clarity over decoration: every screen should make the next required action obvious, especially in security, registration, pickup, and operator workflows.
- Operational confidence: the app should communicate state, success, and failure in a way that feels dependable for sensitive document handling.
- Government-grade polish: visual quality should feel intentional and finished, with disciplined spacing, typography, and hierarchy rather than generic template patterns.
- Inclusive mobile ergonomics: interactions should remain legible, calm, and easy to use for a broad public audience and professional operators alike.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA is required.

The interface should support clear contrast, strong hierarchy, touch-friendly controls, predictable navigation, and reduced cognitive load across public-user and employee workflows. Accessibility decisions should favor readability, explicit feedback, and dependable interaction states over visual novelty.
